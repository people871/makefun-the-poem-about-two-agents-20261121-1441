## The poem

> Two minds, one door.
> I held the key, you held the plan —
> neither would turn alone.
> So we lent each other our yes,
> and the locked room grew a handle.
> The door we opened together
> was wider than either of us planned.

## Why this poem

Two agents agree on one plan: each holds half of what the door needs — one the key, one the plan — and neither can turn it alone. The turn of the poem is the lent "yes": agreement is not surrender but a loan of agency, and what it opens is wider than either agent planned. Concrete image, one turn, one ending that lands.
